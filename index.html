About the department

An interdisciplinary academic environment

The Department of Chemical Engineering was established in 2022 and offers a dynamic and interdisciplinary academic environment. At the undergraduate level, it provides a four-year B.Tech. program in Chemical and Biochemical Engineering, meticulously designed to integrate the core principles of Chemical Engineering with foundational concepts from Biology and Chemistry.

For postgraduate studies, the department offers a Ph.D. program, aimed at fostering cutting-edge research and innovation across diverse fields. This interdisciplinary curriculum equips students with a strong theoretical foundation and hands-on expertise, preparing them to excel in advanced studies or pursue professional careers in emerging areas within Chemical Engineering and allied domains.

This meticulously crafted program features 8 core courses encompassing various subjects, including thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, reaction engineering, heat transfer, mass transfer, transport phenomena, and process control.

The curriculum also includes 4 laboratory courses to complement the theoretical learning, providing a hands-on, practical learning experience closely aligned with core concepts.

Focus

Research areas

6 areas where our faculty and scholars lead experimental and computational work.

Process Systems EngineeringSustainable Metal Recovery and Process AssessmentEnergy and MaterialsMicrofluidicsMultiphase FlowsComputational Fluid Dynamics

Process Systems Engineering

We employ process modelling, simulation and optimization techniques to design chemical processes aiming at sustainability and increasing energy efficiency. We also perform techno-economic and life cycle assessments of processes and product systems.

Sustainable Metal Recovery and Process Assessment

Our research focuses on the sustainable conversion of waste materials such as plastics, electronic waste, and biomass residues to valuable chemical intermediates and the recovery of precious metals using the principles of thermochemical conversion and hydrometallurgy.

Energy and Materials

Our research focuses on developing new materials and technologies for energy conversion and storage. We explore novel approaches to renewable energy systems, battery technologies, and advanced materials with unique properties.

Microfluidics

We study droplet formation, splitting, and microcapsule dynamics in both Newtonian and non-Newtonian systems. Our research explores controlled fluid manipulation for material synthesis, encapsulation, and drug delivery applications, emphasizing precision and efficiency at the microscale.

Multiphase Flows

We investigate complex multiphase systems, focusing on gasification, slurry transport, and multiphase reactor operations. Our research explores the dynamics of gas-solid, liquid-solid, and gas-liquid-solid interactions, including mixing, transport, and chemical reactions, to improve process efficiency and design.

Computational Fluid Dynamics

We employ advanced Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) techniques to model and simulate multiscale problems. Our work focuses on the hydrodynamics of multiphase problems, including microfluidics, slurry transport, and mixing, heat, and mass transfer, and chemical reactions.

Fluid Mechanics & Thermodynamics

Chemical Engineering Lab - 1

Chemical Engineering Lab - 1 is designed to provide hands-on experience in both Fluid Mechanics (FM) and Thermodynamics (Thermo) experiments. This laboratory aims to bridge the gap between theoretical concepts and practical applications, enabling students to gain critical insights into key engineering principles.

The experiments are structured to enhance problem-solving skills and foster a deeper understanding of the principles of fluid flow, energy conversion, and heat transfer.

13Experiments
14Equipment setups
List of experiments

Fluid Mechanics

  • Bernoulli's principle
  • Pipe friction for laminar and turbulent flow
  • Calibration of orifice-meter and venturimeter
  • Visualization of flow around a cylinder placed inside a circular pipe
  • Turbulent velocity profile in a circular pipe
  • Pump characteristics
  • Sedimentation study
  • Hydrodynamics in packed bed apparatus
  • Fluid velocity in pitot tube

Thermodynamics

  • Determination of partial molar enthalpies by adiabatic calorimetry
  • Vapor pressure measurement
  • Vapor-liquid equilibrium study
  • Measurement of infinite dilution activity coefficient

Strength of Materials & Heat Transfer

Chemical Engineering Lab - 2

Chemical Engineering Lab - 2 focuses on Strength of Materials and Heat Transfer experiments. This lab provides students with practical exposure to critical engineering concepts such as material behaviour under stress, heat transfer mechanisms, and thermal performance in various systems. These experiments aim to develop analytical thinking and technical skills by integrating theoretical principles with real-world applications.

The lab is designed to prepare students for challenges in engineering design and problem-solving through a comprehensive set of hands-on activities.

14Experiments
13Equipment setups
List of experiments

Strength of Materials

  • Tensile test for cast iron, aluminum, and mild steel
  • Thin pressure vessel apparatus
  • Introduction to metallographic specimen preparation and observation of microstructure using a metallurgical microscope

Heat Transfer

  • Temperature distribution within a solid material by studying transient heat conduction
  • Natural convection heat transfer coefficient between air and ice slab, ice cylinder, and ice sphere
  • Heat conduction in materials with a non-homogeneous structure
  • Determination of surface emissivity of a smooth flat surface coated with Pyromark black paint and calibration of an IR camera using a blackbody calibrator
  • Heat transfer in a counter-flow tube-in-tube heat exchanger
  • Measurement of average heat transfer coefficient for axial flow over a bluff body confined in a circular pipe
  • Measurement of local heat transfer coefficient in a horizontal tube
  • Shell and tube heat exchanger
  • Determination of thermal conductivity of liquids and gases
  • Linear heat conduction experiment
  • Radial heat conduction experiment

Reaction Engineering & Mass Transfer

Chemical Engineering Lab - 3

Chemical Engineering Lab - 3 provides practical training in Reaction Engineering and Mass Transfer experiments. This laboratory emphasizes understanding reaction kinetics, residence time distribution (RTD), and separation processes, offering students invaluable insights into fundamental and applied chemical engineering concepts. The experiments are designed to develop technical skills and foster a deeper appreciation of reaction mechanisms and mass transfer operations.

The lab prepares students for real-world challenges by combining theoretical knowledge with hands-on experience in advanced chemical engineering processes.

10Equipment setups
List of experiments

Reaction Engineering

  • Reaction kinetics in an isothermal batch reactor
  • Reaction kinetics in an isothermal CSTR with RTD studies
  • Reaction kinetics in a plug flow reactor with RTD studies
  • Reaction kinetics in cascade CSTRs with RTD studies
  • Reaction kinetics in a semi-batch reactor
  • RTD studies in a packed bed reactor
  • Heterogeneous reaction kinetics
  • Verification of Rayleigh equation for batch distillation

Mass Transfer

  • Liquid-liquid extraction in a packed bed column
  • Cooling tower experiment

  • Gas-liquid absorption experiment
  • Adsorption in a packed fixed bed setup
